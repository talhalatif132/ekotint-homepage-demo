# Paint reviews: product feedback and company experiences

14 November 2026, Asia/Karachi. Research only; no application changes, tests/build, push or deployment.

The user asked whether paint-industry reviews belong to a product or the overall company, and explicitly requested an internet check after the product Reviews tab was hidden. The earlier “hide it” instruction remains in effect; this research is not approval to restore it. Source/data/renderers remain retained.

## Sources checked

- https://www.behr.com/products/paint/behr-marquee-interior — official MARQUEE product page has product reviews, a submission action, product-quality/value ratings and labels identifying the original paint variant. Direct evidence of product-scoped feedback; no exact rating/count is relied on.
- https://www.sherwin-williams.com/homeowners/products/superpaint-interior-acrylic-latex/650273782?replacement=true — official SuperPaint page includes product rating/review controls and Write a Review. Dynamic count/rating placeholders did not resolve in extracted text, so no numerical rating/count is asserted.
- https://www.clare.com/pages/all-reviews — official general reviews landing page highlights a customer experience covering coverage, shades and customer service. Supports a wider testimonial presentation; the extracted text does not reveal every review's underlying product association or submission model.
- https://www.trustpilot.com/review/coatpaints.com — company profile contains customer experiences about paint, delivery and service. A company review platform can include product comments; it is not a dedicated rating of one paint. Do not infer Trustpilot integration into Ekotint or treat this as proof of COAT's own-site placement.

These examples demonstrate both review types; this is a bounded review of published pages, not an industry-wide prevalence survey or a submission/moderation-service audit.

## Recommendation for Ekotint

Keep specific paint feedback (coverage, finish, application, durability) attached to that paint. Use genuine broader customer experiences (advice, ordering, delivery, service) for homepage testimonials. If a product review is featured on the homepage, retain the product name/context; do not silently turn product scores into a company rating. Both types can coexist.

Keep the product Reviews tab hidden under the current user instruction. Restore only if requested when genuine product feedback is available or a real review-collection journey is commissioned. Current `homeCompanyReviews` and `homeProductReviews` are empty; the homepage's nine fictional preview testimonials are separate design samples, not genuine customer evidence. Replace samples with actual customer feedback for a real customer-facing review section. Review submission/storage remains a previously agreed service deferral.

No UI task or fresh Sol validation was required for this research. Existing source publication restrictions remain.
