## Original generated hero

`hero-room.png`: 1536 × 1024 PNG. Created using the built-in `image_gen.imagegen` tool and visually inspected. A fictional editorial architectural scene, not a photograph of an Ekotint installation. No external reference images were used.

The broad main olive wall is suitable for a prepared-scene wall-color preview, with an explicit wall mask excluding the sofa, stair and adjacent walls. Any preview remains an approximation; generated scene colors are not calibrated physical paint swatches.

Original generated file: `/Users/sam/.codex/generated_images/01a16947-1b82-7e50-ace7-b46bcfd430b8/exec-1fe62bc3-3a2a-495a-b0f9-e8b0954b31e2.png`. A copy is persisted in this project so the demo does not depend on that external path.

Final prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural
> Asset type: original wide 3:2 architectural editorial hero photograph for a premium Pakistani paint company website.
> Primary request: a beautifully composed contemporary Pakistani villa living room with warm late-afternoon sunlight, a large pale sage and olive painted wall, cream sculptural sofa, walnut furniture, natural limestone floor, restrained styling and one tasteful indoor plant.
> Composition: wide landscape 3:2 image, professionally photographed at human eye level with a natural 28mm architectural lens, corrected verticals. Large softly shadowed deeper olive wall region on the left is clear and quiet, suitable for overlaying white headline text. The cream sculptural sofa sits across the lower middle-right, a low walnut table with two simple ceramics, tall window on the far right with warm sunlight creating a beautiful geometric pool of light on limestone. A single indoor plant near the window. Calm premium contemporary South Asian home; refined and believable, not generic luxury hotel.
> Materials: tactile smooth matt paint, cream linen upholstery, warm walnut grain, honed pale natural limestone. Subtle imperfections and realistic light falloff, elegant editorial photography.
> Constraints: no text, no logos, no people, no watermark, no ornate decor, no extra plants, no chandeliers, no clutter, no overprocessed HDR, no illustration or CGI appearance.
